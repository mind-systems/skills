## What a handoff is, and what it stops being

A handoff is a **snapshot of memory** — where you are, what is in flight, what the durable artifacts do not hold. Not an inventory of the session, not a summary of the work. Write it that way and it stays short, because everything else already lives on disk.

The processed mark is retired. It shipped this morning and by evening the evidence was against it: agents refuse to flip it or simply forget, and handoff 19 — spent, superseded, known to be both — still carries `[ ]` because the architect who superseded it did not mark it. A rule nobody follows is not a rule, and chasing compliance costs more than the state it was meant to record. Do not mark a handoff, do not expect a mark, and read no meaning into one you find: the marks already on disk are residue, not signal.
