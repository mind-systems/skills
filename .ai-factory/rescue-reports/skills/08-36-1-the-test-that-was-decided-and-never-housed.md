# 36.1 — the test that was decided and never housed

**Project:** skills
**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Stopped at:** planned:3
**Elapsed before the rescue:** 2481

The plan carried into this attempt was the one the previous rescue kept. It states each of the specification's rulings three times — once as context, once as the instruction to follow, once as a list of things to confirm afterwards. What follows is what happened to those three copies.

The first reading found the copies already disagreeing on the specification's most explicit ruling. The specification splits one sentence of `agent-architect` into three clauses and rules on each, saying of the third that its stated reason does not survive with it — the buffer being "not a shared artifact" stops being true once the editor holds the settled zone. The plan instructed the opposite, told the implementer to keep that reason, and wrote it into its own confirmation list, so the check would have ratified the contradiction rather than caught it. The same reading found the plan justifying a boundary on the claim that no document says how the editor learns which buffer is its own — which the governing spec had stated that same morning — and denying that a neighbouring task reaches the same sentence, leaving that neighbour's clause unguarded inside a paragraph this task rewraps.

The second reading found all three repaired at their cause, and two new disagreements among the same three copies. The change the contract line names as the task's headline — assigning the architect's recorded items to a zone — was pinned in the context, shaped nowhere in the instruction, and absent from the confirmation list: the two changes the contract line and the specification name most explicitly were the two the verification did not check. The guard added by the first repair could not pass as written, because it demanded byte-identity from a clause hard-wrapped inside the very paragraph the task rewraps.

The third reading found both of those repaired, and three more. Two were again disagreements between the instruction and the confirmation list: the instruction widens `editor.md`'s reason for loading the engine to cover the buffer's rules, while the confirmation list forbids the word "buffer" from occurring in that file at all — a ban whose only source is a neighbouring specification's description of the file as it stands today, read as though it were a constraint. A third notes that cutting the buffer's path from the architect leaves the file's own "records your buffer's path (below)" resolving to a pointer rather than to a path, one hop short of what it promises.

The remaining finding is not about the copies. The specification decides a test for sorting whatever the architect parks in the buffer — a known defect, a settled ruling, or a fact the hand needs in order to act on one side; an open question or a forming diagnosis on the other — and states that this is decided there and not left to whoever rewords the privacy sentence. It then gives that test no home. The engine's change list is five items, and what it grants is the two zones and what each holds: two characterisations, taken from the governing spec, which states characterisations and no test. The architect is told to point at the engine rather than restate anything. So the architect points at a test that was never written, and the decision the specification calls its own lands in no file. The reading before this one saw the same gap in its earlier form and called it an assignment left to the implementer to shape.

That test is not in the governing spec, and it does not violate it — it is an addition. It was written into this specification by the previous rescue of this same task, correcting an earlier draft that had sorted entries by category. The correction was right about the sorting and silent about where the rule would live.

> The specification decided a test for sorting what the buffer holds and gave it no home — granting the engine two characterisations and forbidding the architect to restate anything, so the pointer it requires points at a rule the change list never writes.

Root-cause category: specification gap, of the kind that decides something and houses it nowhere. Recurring: the zone test's homelessness, in two consecutive readings.

## What was done

Repaired at specification depth, with a full reset. The test goes, and the zone assignment with it: the task no longer assigns anything to a zone, and the specification now states that the architect goes on naming what it records — the editor's handle, any assigned pairing role, its deferral entries — and labels none of them, because the engine states what each zone holds and the architect loads that engine at birth. The contract line loses the same sentence and nothing else.

Two items the blast radius did not enumerate were added: the reference reading "records your buffer's path (below)", which the task must reword once the path moves, and the permission for `editor.md` to name the buffer in the engine's own word, which a neighbouring specification's description of that file had been read as forbidding.

The plan was deleted with its three readings and its status file, though the previous rescue had kept it. That is the reverse of the choice made on this same task earlier the same day, and the readings themselves supply the reason: five of the six findings raised after the first reading were disagreements between the plan's instruction and the plan's own confirmation list, not defects in the change. A plan repaired a fourth time repairs a copy; a plan written fresh states each ruling once.

One observation recurred in all three readings and belongs to no task in the phase. The engine will tell the editor to hold a settled zone whose content — the working discipline, the user's rulings about how the pair works, the method learned from repeated failure, the register of where the skills lag — the architect is nowhere told to write. The zone the hand is given to hold is one the head has no instruction to fill. It survives this rescue unowned, recorded here because the readings that carried it were deleted with the plan.
