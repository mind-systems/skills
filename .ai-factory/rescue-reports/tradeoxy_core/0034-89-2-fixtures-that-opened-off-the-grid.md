# 89.2 — fixtures that opened off the grid

**Project:** tradeoxy_core
**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Stopped at:** escalated
**Elapsed before the rescue:** 607
**Sessions:** planner: 827fed77-3fd8-417c-9fa3-51402cc8adcf, plan-reviewer-1: 63e587ea-0b6a-4777-b0b2-649342b303fd, plan-reviewer-2: 5f216d50-8224-40fe-a79b-b3e7bfc32383, implementer: 04e61597-f742-43c7-94ad-b4d4b0ae69ea

The plan did what the task asked. Three places stamp a closed bar: the live close, a slot's history and the replayer. Each now takes the close from the grid, so a month bar closes at the start of the following month. The plan named the seam between this task and the next one, where the live fold moves onto the grid. A bar opened off the grid gets the end of the grid bar its open falls in. The plan also stated that no existing assertion touches it: «cases with off-grid opens at `1700000000` never assert the stamp». The first plan review raised two small points in the month cases. The second passed the plan and noted that the spec wrongly said a month's `cursorTimestamp` follows the stamp. Only the event's close moves, and the alert's fire date with it; the cursor comes from the bar's open.

The implementer made the change, and every suite passed except one. `backtest-runtime.spec.ts` replays the recorded indicator scenarios through the live close and compares alert `createdAt` byte for byte with their recorded expectations, and four of its cases failed. The scenario generator, `src/indicators/__fixtures__/scenarios/generate.ts`, builds every scenario from `STUB_OPEN_TIME = 1_700_000_000_000`, which is 20 seconds off the minute grid. It records the close as open plus width, `1700000060`. The grid's close for that open is `1700000040`. The implementer stopped and offered three ways forward:
- move the scenarios onto the grid and regenerate their expectations;
- feed grid-aligned opens to the live close in that comparison only;
- leave the failures until the next task.

The governing spec, `docs/candle-pipeline/what-a-bar-is.md` § «A bar has a kind», says «A time bar — fixed, dynamic or calendar, of any width — opens and closes on the grid». The change does not violate it; the fixtures do, because they describe bars that cannot exist.

> Decide whether the recorded indicator scenarios move onto the grid, with the generator starting on a minute boundary and the scenarios and their expectations regenerated, or stay off it.

## What was done

The user chose to move the scenarios onto the grid (escalation option 1).
- **Spec `0330` and the 89.2 contract line** now say that the generator starts at `STUB_OPEN_TIME = 1_699_999_980_000`, a minute boundary, and that every scenario JSON is regenerated by `generate.ts`. They name `backtest-runtime.spec.ts`, `backtest-scenarios.spec.ts` and the harness as what breaks on contact, and they correct the `cursorTimestamp` claim.
- **Full reset:** the plan, both plan reviews and the sidecar were deleted.
- **Working tree:** the implementer's code changes were reverted to HEAD at the user's choice: `indicator-runtime.service.ts` and its spec, `slot-axis.ts`, the replayer and its spec, and the new `slot-axis.spec.ts`. The orchestrator re-plans from a clean tree.
