### Findings

1. **"Four changes" is contradicted by the five items under it** — § "The engine — where the buffer is defined", second task: "Four changes inside § \"The architect's buffer\", and nothing else in the section:" is followed by items 1–5. This is a census over a list the list itself falsifies (`docs/counts-go-stale.md`). The concrete failure: an implementer who treats the count as the checklist stops after item 4 and leaves the closing paragraph's ", settled zone included" in place — a surviving zone reference in the engine, which the verification sweep would then fail on. Fix: drop the count, or write "Five changes". Nothing else in the item list needs to move.

2. **The plan bolds words the spec pins unbolded, in the one file where bold is plausible.** Items 3 and 4 quote the replacements as "The editor re-reads the **memory** when it changes" and "that **the memory** has moved". The spec's pinned wording has no emphasis there, and the paragraph being deleted two lines above uses exactly this markup for real (`The **settled** zone`, `The **live** zone`) — so `**memory**` reads as file text, not as the plan pointing at its own change. An implementer copying the quoted sentence literally lands `**memory**` in the engine body and introduces emphasis that the spec does not pin and that the section no longer has any reason to carry. The read-back step will not catch it, since it compares words. Fix: strip the emphasis from the two quotes and say in prose which word changed.

3. **Two measurements do not match the files.** (a) The `agent-architect` replacement fragment "it is where your buffer's path and rules are defined, so it is" is **62** characters, not the 63 the plan states; (b) "inside the range the file's other lines already use (63–75)" does not describe this file — its mid-paragraph lines run from 38 to 76 columns, and the neighbouring paragraph alone spans 65–72. The decision the numbers are offered in support of (don't rewrap) is correct and matters for 59.2's disjointness, but an implementer who verifies the figure finds a one-character discrepancy in a task where a missing character *is* the defect class, and has to rule on whether their own copy is short a word. Fix: state the decision without the arithmetic, or correct both figures. The same looseness appears in the engine instruction "stay inside the file's existing wrap width (~78 columns)" while the pinned block's second line is 79 columns with its indent — matching the file's existing 79-column lines, so the block is right and the bound is what is off.
