# Plan: 37.1 — the architect's outgoing communication is misdescribed as a closed list, twice over

## Context
`src/skills/agent-architect/SKILL.md` claims in three places that everything the architect ever sends the editor is one of two things: the section heading "## Relay on the marker; author a prompt in exactly one case", the body sentence "You author your own prompt in exactly one case: the **apply work-order**, once the user has confirmed the edits.", and the closing inventory "Two channel-message formats, nothing else: …". All three are false — the architect also authors `REPORT-ONLY` rounds on its own initiative to delegate its own legwork, and per `docs/paired-loop.md` § "What crosses the channel" the two formats govern only what *opens a round*, not the ordinary communication that keeps the hand current. This task names both authored cases in the heading and the body, disambiguates the pronoun the widened body sentence would strand, and rescopes the "nothing else" sentence to what it actually governs. One file changes.
