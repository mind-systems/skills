# Phase 78 — the hand writes its own snapshot and a new hand carries on from it

[paired-loop](../../../docs/paired-loop.md) says "The snapshot that continues the memory is the head's to write, for the same reason: only whoever held the conversation can report on what happened in it" (§ "What the memory holds, and who holds it"), and calls a snapshot "a head continuing itself" (§ "How the memory begins, and how it survives"). It holds that a hand "is asked to read, never to recall", since "a hand's own history ends without a signal". It says nothing of a hand's own snapshot, of a fresh hand continuing from one, or of a folder split by writer. The doc is amended first, docs before skills.

Where the skills say the opposite, or less:
- **`agent-architect`**, § "Spawn once, message thereafter": the snapshot "is never delegated to the editor to compose"; § "Relay on the marker; author the apply work-order and your own legwork" calls composing it "forbidden to the editor outright".
- **A fresh hand appears only after a dead one**, same first section: "one spawn, then every subsequent round goes into the same conversation via `SendMessage` — never a fresh spawn per task", and "If the send fails, the editor is dead".
- **`architect-editor-engine`**, § "The architect's buffer": the folder holds `buffer.md`, `address.md` and "the head's own snapshots", numbered inside the folder, with no `architect/` or `editor/`.
- **`src/agents/editor.md`**: the editor is "born fresh into this role at every spawn" and holds the buffer "as your own working context"; it names no file it may write beyond a work-order's edits, and says of its own memory only "You carry history across rounds" and "But never depend on it", with "a fresh editor picking up from the next round alone must succeed just the same".

The split this phase rests on is mechanism and policy. The engine already holds where snapshots live and how they are numbered. With two writers it also takes the snapshot as a genre, which today lives in `agent-architect`: "the volatile residue" and "the reasoning that shaped it", "thick by default", one that "supersedes the last by name", written only by whoever held the context. It also takes `architect/` and `editor/`, each numbered on its own. What stays with each caller is policy: when (the head before a compact or on request, the hand when the head decides to replace it), what about (the head about the conversation, the hand about its own work, the files it knows, traps in the material, its own errors), and what each reads at start (the head its buffer and its latest snapshot in `architect/`, a new hand the buffer and the latest in `editor/`).

One home per fact: the head's snapshot carries "what the hand knows", and § "Spawn once, message thereafter" has the head keep "a digest of what the editor has accumulated", written from outside. Once the hand writes its own snapshot, that section is a link to the hand's latest, not a retelling.

When the head knows to act: it sees the hand's context size in the notification that closes each round. The window for a replacement is the user's decision: from half the hand's context, a firm floor, no replacement before it; to four fifths, a soft mark, to aim to have replaced by then and not a line to police; between the two, the head picks the seam. The replacement is the head's quiet act and never a topic for the user: no token counts, no forecasts, no asking; like a dead hand, it is named in one line in passing. No doc or skill states any of this yet; none of the texts above mentions context size, a seam, a window or when a hand is replaced.

What a hand reported from inside a compact that came in the middle of an order:
- **A compact comes with a signal.** The hand's turn opens with "This session is being continued from a previous conversation that ran out of context", followed by a summary and a pointer to the full transcript. [paired-loop](../../../docs/paired-loop.md) says "A hand's own history ends without a signal" (§ "What the memory holds, and who holds it"); for a compact that does not hold, and the doc is amended with the rest of the phase.
- **What a compact keeps and loses.** Files the hand had read can survive verbatim. The order survives only "in substance": the hand cannot tell the order's own words from the summary's gloss. A text the order pinned verbatim was lost, and the hand composed its own in its place, which the head caught only by verifying against the file. Pinned values are what the pair's reliability rests on, and they are what a compact loses; that is why a hand is replaced before it compacts, not after.
- **After a compact the hand did not re-read the buffer.** The summary said it had, and the hand trusted the summary. A compact can still come before the head replaces the hand, so a hand that finds itself continued from a summary re-reads the buffer before acting.
