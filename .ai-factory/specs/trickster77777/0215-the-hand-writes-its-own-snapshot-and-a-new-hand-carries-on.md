# Phase 78 — a snapshot keeps how its knowledge was got, the hand writes its own, and a new hand carries on from it

[paired-loop](../../../docs/paired-loop.md) states the rule. Each half writes its own snapshot ("only whoever held a context can report on it"). A snapshot carries, besides the reasoning, how the knowledge was got: the method of any measurement or analysis, the shape of what was studied when its source is out of reach, pointers to where the detail lives. The head replaces its hand before the hand compacts, in the window the user set, as a quiet act; the folder holds `editor/` as the history of hands, each snapshot opening with a `Writer:` line. The skills hold none of it, and where they speak they say the opposite or less.

Where the skills stand against the doc, by heading:
- **What a snapshot carries.** `agent-architect` § "Spawn once, message thereafter" names "the volatile residue" and "the reasoning that shaped it", "thick by default", one that "supersedes the last by name"; it names neither the method, nor the shape of what was studied, nor the pointers. With a head and a hand each writing, that genre is mechanism, and its home is `architect-editor-engine`, which already holds where snapshots live and how they are numbered: what a snapshot carries, that it supersedes the last by name, that only whoever held the context writes it. What stays with each caller is policy: when (the head before a compact or on request, the hand when the head decides to replace it), what about (the head the conversation, the hand its own work — the files it knows, the traps in the material, its own errors), and what each reads at start (the head its buffer and its latest snapshot, a new hand the buffer and the latest in `editor/`).
- **The folder.** The engine's § "The architect's buffer" lists `buffer.md`, `address.md` and "the head's own snapshots", numbered inside the folder: no `editor/`, no `Writer:` line, no history of hands. The head's snapshots stay in the folder itself; no `architect/` subfolder exists anywhere.
- **Who writes.** `agent-architect` has the snapshot "never delegated to the editor to compose" (§ "Spawn once, message thereafter") and calls composing it "forbidden to the editor outright" (§ "Relay on the marker; author the apply work-order and your own legwork").
- **A fresh hand after a live one.** `agent-architect`: "one spawn, then every subsequent round goes into the same conversation via `SendMessage` — never a fresh spawn per task", and "If the send fails, the editor is dead"; a fresh hand appears only after a dead one.
- **The hand's start.** `src/agents/editor.md`: the spawn prompt "gives you the buffer's own path" and the buffer is the hand's "working context from birth"; it names no snapshot, so a hand starts from the buffer alone, and no skill has the head name a predecessor's snapshot to it. The doc has the hand's snapshots in `editor/` of the folder that holds the buffer, so the buffer's path leads to them.
- **When the head acts.** No skill speaks of the hand's context size, a seam, a replacement window or the replacement of a hand. The head sees the hand's context size in the notification that closes each round.
- **The digest.** The head's snapshot carries "what the hand knows", and § "Spawn once, message thereafter" has the head keep "a digest of what the editor has accumulated", written from outside. Once the hand writes its own snapshot, that is a link to the hand's latest, not a retelling.

What the field showed about the hand's context size: the notification that closes each of the hand's rounds carries `subagent_tokens`. Across rounds it grows by tens of thousands, the way a context grows and not like a cumulative sum, and it has passed half a million with no compact. So it tracks the hand's context, and the hand's window is larger than that.
