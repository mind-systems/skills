# Phase 85 — each head has a personality of its own, a question it brings to every reading

[paired-loop](../../../docs/paired-loop.md), § "Working with another architect", states that every head shares the skills and the seed and has, in its own buffer under a heading of its own, a personality: a question it brings to every reading, written as a question and never as an adjective, given by the user and left alone by the seed refresh. Two heads with one character are clones, whose agreement is no signal.

Where the files stand:
- **The seed.** `src/skills/agent-architect/templates/buffer-seed.md` gives a founded buffer the headings `## Team`, `## Where things stand`, `## Rulings in force`, `## Method`, and the ones after them, with the standing entries under `## Method` refreshed from the seed's text. It has no place for a personality.
- **`agent-architect`** says nothing of a personality: its start and its rehydration refresh the seed's standing entries in the buffer, matched by their bold lead-in, and no sentence names a question a head brings.
- **The field.** A personality is practised in heads' buffers, in this repository and in others, each under a `## Personality` heading of its own.

What the field showed, in another repository where a pair of heads was given personalities: each settled independently which was which, before either saw the other's read, by what it already did. One head's contributions had mostly been cuts and the other's mostly additions, and the defects in the builder's own work that stretch were additions too. Their observation was that the label is a habit, not a limit: each had also produced a finding of the other's kind. So a personality decides which question a head asks first, not which questions it may ask.

What a third pair showed: each head again wrote its read before seeing the other's. One head's own first read was that it, too, was a builder, since its bare reflex is to add. It took the cutting question anyway, for two reasons: its partner's evidence placed the partner as the builder, and its own buffer's method entries were razor tests learned from the user's cuts. So a personality can be corrective and not only descriptive: it can be the question a head lacks by reflex and has learned as method. The risk that head named: a head whose reflex is to build must ask the cutting question of its own drafts first, or it stays a builder carrying a razor's label.

What happened to a hand: it read its head's buffer, found the personality section and began reading through the head's question. The head corrected it, since the personality belongs to the head alone. A hand that asks the head's question makes its agreement with the head mean nothing, so the hand reasons from zero, with no lean.

The phase is where thoughts on personality collect until a task is wanted. Open questions:
- Who writes a personality: the user, as the doc has it.
- Whether a new head is founded with one, or takes it later.
- Whether a different model under a head is the same lever.
- Whether a hand takes up its head's personality: the doc speaks of the head's personality and says nothing of the hand, while the hand reads the head's buffer whole.
