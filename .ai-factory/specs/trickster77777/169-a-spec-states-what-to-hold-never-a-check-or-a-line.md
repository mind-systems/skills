# Phase 61 — a spec states what to hold, never a check or a line

No governing document exists for this phase.

Two of the user's rulings have lived in `.ai-factory/notes/07-architect-buffer.md` § "Rulings, in the user's own terms" for weeks, each recorded there as a debt against a skill and never drained into an artifact. The first: "A task is not an instruction for reviewing itself. The orchestrator has a department for that. Guards against what nobody would do, and counts that restate the instruction, belong to neither tier." The second, in the user's own words: "Мы должны объяснять 'в таком-то классе нужны методы такого плана', не надо объяснять на какой строке что написать" — a task explains what an artifact must hold, never where to type it. Checked directly: `grep -rln "review of itself\|reviewing itself\|guards against what nobody would do\|department for that" docs/ src/` and `grep -rln "where to type\|which line to\|line to write\|where in the file to" docs/ src/` both return nothing. Neither ruling is stated anywhere under `docs/` or `src/`.

The cost of the absence showed up in this stretch's own work: `command-pin-gaps.md`'s Blast-radius holes clause ordered its invariant as "every match must satisfy after the change" — an instruction to a later run — and task 58.2's rescue paid for it, its plan carrying four verification tasks against two edit tasks, every one of three failed rounds landing inside the verification half, none ever faulting the change itself. The rule that would have prevented it was already sitting in the buffer while the clause that produced it was written.

`src/skills/roadmap-engine/SKILL.md`'s "What a task spec holds" paragraph is the nearest existing text to either ruling — "three parts and nothing else" — and it was not enough: nothing there says what those three parts exclude, so a fourth part, a checking instruction predicting a later run's output, arrived without ever contradicting the letter. Phase 61 drains both rulings. `61.1` is task `56.3` moved here unchanged — command-pin-gaps.md's own Repair sentence stops ordering a check for later and becomes a recorded finding instead, per the first ruling. `61.2` reaches the root: the "What a task spec holds" paragraph itself gains a sentence naming what "nothing else" excludes — no check on whether the instruction was carried out, since that is the review the orchestrator already runs, and no part naming a position in the file rather than what the artifact must hold — the second ruling stated at the root many callers read, `command-pin-gaps` among them by name.

Nothing under `docs/` is written.
