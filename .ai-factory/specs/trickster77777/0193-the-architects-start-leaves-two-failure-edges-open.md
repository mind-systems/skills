# Phase 69 — a failed probe and a dead hand holding a relay have a defined outcome

**Touches:** 2

[paired-loop](../../../docs/paired-loop.md) § "Where the memory lives" says "A session no folder claims is a new head, which founds its own folder, seeds its buffer, and writes its keeper; a brand-new chat never adopts an existing folder, however it is asked or whatever it is handed." It says nothing of a session whose probe of its own id fails. Two places in `agent-architect` leave an outcome open, and each needs the user's ruling.

**A failed probe.** § "Spawn once, message thereafter" finds a folder by the `session-id:` line of `address.md` and says "No folder found, for whatever reason, means you are a new head — you never ask the user which". Its probe paragraph says "When the probe does not yield exactly one file, write no session id and carry on: leave `address.md` as it was, or unwritten at a founding". Together they leave two cases. A head whose probe fails at its founding leaves a folder with no `session-id:` line, which no later start finds, so a head founded again leaves the first buffer and its snapshots orphaned. A resumed head whose probe fails once, for instance because the nonce it printed is not yet in the transcript, finds no folder and founds a new one beside its own. The "as it was" branch of the probe paragraph can no longer occur, since a folder is found only through a successful probe. The only signal is the passing mention that the id could not be read. No document says whether a folder founded with an unreadable id should become recoverable, or whether a failed probe is retried before a head is founded.

**A dead hand holding a relay.** The dead-editor paragraph in the same section asks that "the next order you compose **must be** self-contained in its own right". § "Relay on the marker; author the apply work-order and your own legwork" says a user's relay is forwarded as it stands, "enrichment is never yours to initiate on your own judgment". When the editor dies while the head holds an unforwarded `::` payload, the payload is the user's own words and a fresh hand lacks the ground it assumed; the head either forwards it unenriched or pins context into it and breaks the no-enrichment rule. Neither the paragraph nor any document says which.
