# Phase 70 — the buffer's contents and its copying are stated where the buffer is defined

**Touches:** 2

[paired-loop](../../../docs/paired-loop.md) § "Where the pair's behaviour lives" says the seed a buffer is founded from carries the pair's behaviour into every new buffer as standing entries. No document says in full what a buffer holds or what copying the seed "whole" carries over, and the three places that define the buffer disagree at the edges.

**The editor's handle.** `architect-editor-engine` § "The architect's buffer" says the buffer "holds the working discipline, the rulings the user has made about how the pair works, the method learned from repeated failure, and the register of where the skills still lag the practice", and names no handle. `agent-architect` § "Spawn once, message thereafter" has the head "write its handle into the buffer", and § "Your buffer is shared; you alone write it" names "the editor's handle and the deferral entries below" as its state. The seed has headings for a head's team, its position, rulings, method, orientation, ledger, candidates and current thread, and none for the handle. A reader taking the engine's sentence as complete could conclude the handle does not belong in the buffer.

**Terms the engine uses and does not define.** The engine's drain paragraph says the base behaviour of the pair "drains to the seed the buffer is founded from and stays in the buffer as its standing entry". Only `agent-architect` and `templates/buffer-seed.md` define the seed and the standing entry, and the editor never loads that skill; it meets both through the entries in the buffer it reads. The engine is the home of the buffer's definition and does not name the seed in it.

**"Copied whole".** The seed's opening says `agent-architect`'s founding step "copies it whole into a new buffer file", and the founding passage says "copied whole", while the opening also says "The headings below are filled over the session, not left as placeholder prose". Copied whole, the seed's own title, its opening paragraph and its angle-bracket guidance lines come along into every new buffer. No document or skill says which of these a founded buffer keeps.
