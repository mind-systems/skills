# Phase 72 — the time entry's evidence is the change, a diff where it has landed and the task where it has not

**Touches:** 2

`polymorphism-philosophy` § "The two entries" says the time entry "takes what a change just brought: does this change bring a second member to a kind, and was a seam cut at that arrival or not — its evidence is the diff". It names the diff as the only evidence, and also says "A consumer skill reaches this unit through the time entry".

The unit has two kinds of reader. Invoked directly, on an area of code or logic or on closed tasks, for analysis, it has a diff to read: the change has landed. Its consumer, `roadmap-decompose-skeleton` Lens 1, "apply[ies] its question to the target task(s)" over open `[ ]` tasks, before any diff exists, and decides whether to cut the seam, with the task text and the current code as its evidence. The question transfers to both; the evidence clause fits only the first, and a run that loads the unit from Lens 1 looks for a diff it cannot have. The user's reading of why the unit is worded so is that it is called both ways, and his own practice shows it: he called the skeleton on a code area and it worked. The evidence is the change itself, a diff where the change has landed and the task that describes it where it has not, and both readers stay.

No document governs the unit's wording; a skill is its own documentation, and none is written.

What leaves the phase. The first finding routed onto it, that `command-pin-gaps`' blast-radius floor cannot be met by a sweep for wording the task removes, describes text that is gone. The reviewer behind it read the clause as it then stood, "naming, at minimum, the task's own target as a result the sweep must return", a later-run invariant. The clause now reads "a recorded finding of what the sweep, run now, reaches", recorded when the spec is written, before the change, while the old wording still sits in the target, so the floor is met.
