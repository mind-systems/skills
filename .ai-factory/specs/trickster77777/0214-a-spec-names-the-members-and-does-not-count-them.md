# Phase 77 — a spec holds no measurement of the tree

[counts-go-stale](../../../docs/counts-go-stale.md) removes from a durable artifact any measurement of the current tree, and its own examples include "how many files a sweep reaches". The steps that write a spec do not say so, and the ones below lean the other way.

- **`roadmap-engine`**, the bold lead-in "What a task spec holds": what is true now is "read from the code with exact values", and its "Nothing else means" list, which names no check, no position and no fence, has no measurement in it.
- **`command-pin-gaps`**, the paragraph "Blast-radius holes": the invariant is "a recorded finding of what the sweep, run now, reaches", written "so a reader can tell a genuinely narrow set from a broken pattern", and "A sweep too large to enumerate is itself a finding". What the sweep reaches is a reading of the tree at that moment, and "narrow" and "too large" are judgements of size, so the step that writes a spec asks for what the doc names as a measurement. The same paragraph says "never the sweep's own enumeration of what it found": it asks for the reading and refuses the list, which leaves the size words as where a count enters.
- **The buffer seed's counts entry**, in `agent-architect/templates/buffer-seed.md`: "in conversation a number or a position is fine", and "A spec least of all carries a number measuring the tree". A work-order is a message in the conversation, so by the entry's wording it sits on the permitted side, though the hand composes the spec from it and its counts reach the spec.

Our own decompose writes counts too: the landed contract lines of 52.1 and 73.1 carry "the three texts are pinned verbatim in the spec". They are history, pruned with their phase.

Where no doc says how it must be:
- The doc's test keeps a count named in the same sentence as a pair ("both doors", "the two gateways"). It does not say whether a count beside a longer list of named members ("these seven logic models", followed by their names) is kept or dropped.
- The doc speaks of a durable artifact and does not say whether a work-order, thrown away once applied, is one.

The phase leaves the doc as it is: its test already catches a measurement, and a work-order's counts are carried by the seed's entry.
