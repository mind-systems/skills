# Phase 42 — the number resolves, and resolves wrongly

A session cited a governing document's section by its number, consistently and wrongly, and the rule it pointed at lived one section over. The user's ruling fixes what a number may address: a number assigned once and split, never shifted, when something is inserted holds like a name — a task's `N.M`, a skill's step — while a heading's ordinal is a position like a line number, so a section of a document is cited by its heading's text. There is no resolver and no link-tracking; what lies on the surface is fixed, and the rest is watched.

[reference-by-name](../../../docs/reference-by-name.md) now says it. § "Granularity, not size" lists "a number assigned once" among the units of naming and says "A heading's ordinal is a position, like a line number, so a section of a document is cited by its heading's text", and § "The check" says of its proximity question that "a heading's ordinal passes it and still shifts when a section is inserted above, so it is a position and the heading's text is its name". Nothing in the document diverges.

What still diverges is the always-loaded rule. The global CLAUDE.md, § "Grounding claims", says "A heading, a bolded rule, a symbol, a numbered item survives every insertion above it; a line number survives none". It lists a numbered item with nothing to tell a number assigned once from a heading's ordinal, so the sentence read in every session still leaves the width the mis-citation went through. The project CLAUDE.md's row for the document above lists "a numbered item" among the units of naming in the same unqualified way. `agent-architect`'s anchor list, "a heading, a bolded rule, a symbol, a unique string — never a position", is a different list for a different purpose, an anchor a match is asserted against, and agrees with the distinction.

What no longer diverges. The bare citations of `orchestrator-artifacts` § 1, § 3 and § 6 in `roadmap-prune` and `task-rescue` are not a divergence: that skill's headings carry their numbers as text (`## 6. Status-marker grammar`) and its sections are numbered like steps, split and not shifted. The three differing member lists the phase first noted are settled: `docs/paired-loop.md` no longer carries its list, the document above states the distinction in place of its own, and the global's is the one that remains.
