# Phase 66 — the skill gives birth to the architect, the buffer is its ROM

Governing spec: `docs/paired-loop.md`

The user's ruling: the behaviour an architect must keep holding lives in its buffer, and the seed is how it gets into every new one. "Он его читает, правит и перечитывает регулярно. Это позволяет ему не забывать и при этом не стараться помнить какие то вещи. А скилл вызывается раз на старте и через 10 сообщений он уже забыл что там было в скиле сказано. Скилл — рождает архитектора, буфер — его пзу." A rule read once does not hold; a rule re-read does. On the hand, in his words: "для редактора это ещё важней, чем для архитектора, тк он пишет." `docs/paired-loop.md` § "Where the pair's behaviour lives" states it: the behaviour both halves must keep holding lives in the buffer and enters every new one through the seed; the drain has two destinations, a project ruling to its artifact and the pair's base behaviour to the seed; at every rehydration the head reads the seed's standing entries against its own and takes the seed's text; what the user said twice, or what failed twice, is a debt to drain.

**Where behaviour lives now.** The seed, `templates/buffer-seed.md`, carries the pair's base behaviour as standing entries under `## Method`: the counts rule, what a spec holds, stating a behaviour and stopping, a task not being retold in the code, and the orchestrator's commit taking the whole tree. `agent-architect` § "Spawn once, message thereafter" copies the seed into a new buffer and reads its standing entries again at every start that finds an existing folder. `architect-editor-engine` § "The architect's buffer" gives the drain two destinations and the said-twice threshold. The hand reads the buffer whole and so holds the entries; it never loads `agent-architect`, and a rule about how an artifact is worded reaches whoever writes it through the buffer.

**The counts entry has no scope.** It opens "A number someone decided is written…" and then "A measurement of the current tree is not written, dated or not", which reads as a ban wherever a number appears. Architects in another repository applied it to plain conversation and stripped numbers from speech. The reason the entry gives, a queued spec going false before the orchestrator reaches it, covers only text read later by someone who cannot ask back; `docs/counts-go-stale.md` scopes itself to "a number in a durable artifact", and the entry does not. The user confirmed the ruling relayed to him: "скилы, это не макросы, которые обязательно надо выполнить, это просто модификаторы поведения." A rule applies where its reason holds.

**Not this phase, by the user's ruling.** Questions to the user put in behaviour (the agent asks in time and rightly), a check before asserting, and a ban on copies of project text in the buffer (an agent writes one to remember and cleans it up later) are not standing entries.
